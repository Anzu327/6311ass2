# Asset and meme provenance
Built-in ImageGen created three empty photographic backgrounds (room / neighborhood street / low-budget gold stage), a headless blue-shirt yellow-shoe dancer, a black/gold microphone host and a white-shirt red-apron sauce cook. They are fictional people, not original creator footage. Genuine PNG alpha is retained in WebP conversion (768×1152, quality88). Existing generated yellow dinosaur is reused only in its own identity-contest family.

Runtime files:
- public/media/remix-{room,street,stage}.webp
- public/media/remix-{dance,host,food}.webp
- public/media/meme-dino.webp
- public/media/demo-portrait.jpg (AI-generated sample face)

Source PNGs and complete prompts are delivered in the creator workspace outputs/for-you-redesign-assets/. Camera face crops are remapped through 32 horizontal strips, then composited into body neck anchors. No original song, clip, character voice or face recording is bundled.

Reference corrections: the diss/Disney homophone refers to a diss of Xie Di, not a lyric performed by Xie Di. Pan's meme is based on a sideways entrance rather than generic swagger. Families are labeled REMIX 1–3, avoiding unverified origin dates. This is an artwork's interpretation, not an authoritative meme archive.

Research:

- https://www.baike.com/wikiid/7353670653218078754 (diss/Disney homophone)

Meme display lettering is a 40KB subset of ZCOOL KuaiLe (SIL OFL1.1), self-hosted to avoid a Google Fonts dependency for Chinese titles. Source: https://github.com/googlefonts/zcool-kuaile . License is bundled at public/media/OFL-ZCOOL.txt; full source font and glyph manifest are retained with workspace assets.
